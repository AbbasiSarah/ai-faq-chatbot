An FAQ chatbot that knows when to ask a human

Try it! This assistant answers questions for Sunrise Tech Academy, a fictional demo business. Ask in English or Roman Urdu, with typos if you like. When it isn't sure, it hands over to a person instead of guessing.

S
Sunrise Tech Academy assistantDemo · answers from the academy's FAQ

How it works. Every FAQ question, plus other ways people ask it, is turned into numbers with TF-IDF, using whole words and 3–4 letter chunks, so "fees", "fee" and "feez" still match. Your question gets the same treatment, and cosine similarity finds the closest FAQ. If the confidence is below 35%, the bot hands the question to a person.

It runs entirely in your browser, with no data sent anywhere. To use it for a real business, swap in their FAQ. It can also be connected to WhatsApp, a website widget, or an AI model for longer answers.
